# Periodic review and reusable learning

Fix the weekly or monthly period, cutoff and decisions under review. Read their saved choices, exact result references and historical company snapshots before current state. Today's balance or a later revision must not replace the original evidence.

Compare intended goals, saved intent, actual or simulated actions, cash changes and outcomes. Separate supporting facts, interpretation and scenario assumptions. Preserve failures; do not invent causes, customers or revenue to complete a story.

Identify changes that affect the next period and propose business choices with outcome goals, bounded commitments and evidence. The Agent client manages tool-level execution. Reports fix their period and cutoff; the service can assign a current baseline, while earlier judgments use explicit historical snapshots.

Only when the user requests durable learning, create or revise an experience Skill. Turn material into usable guidance: when it applies, needed inputs, decision criteria, useful actions, exclusions and review triggers. Preserve the exact decision, report or file versions supporting it.

A single experience supports a limited scope. Simulated outcomes may inform hosting or experimental design, but do not validate real market behavior. Keep customer- or industry-specific conditions. Do not turn accounts, personal identifiers, temporary state or access credentials into reusable instructions.

Read the Skill's current revision before editing, retain history and resolve conflicts. Leave a useful procedure unchanged when there is no new transferable judgment. If persistent learning was not requested, retain conclusions in the report.
