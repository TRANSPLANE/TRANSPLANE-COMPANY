# Cash flow and business reserves

Establish the company mode, accounting currency, review period and cutoff date. Read service metrics and relevant accounts, transactions and subscriptions. Paginate when needed; one page is not the complete ledger.

For missing real bank, payment, invoice or subscription data, request actual material or the minimum source-reading authorization needed. Keep access credentials in the Agent's secure storage or dedicated connection configuration. Label simulated records as fictional; disclose assumptions or retain unknown values when market cost evidence is missing.

Cash combines opening balances and occurred inflows and outflows. Check sources, dates, accounts and currency. Internal transfers must not inflate company net inflow. Separate operating cash from capital contributions and financing. Correct through additional entries or reversals while retaining original evidence.

Where subscription tracking applies, normalize effective fixed subscriptions to MRR; ARR is MRR multiplied by 12. Neither represents collected cash. Exclude free or trial portions, one-off revenue and tax. Do not infer accounting profit from cash flow without sufficient accounting evidence.

Read runway from the service's deterministic definition: available cash divided by average net cash consumption over the last three complete months. Preserve its unavailable or inapplicable state rather than inventing a precise deadline.

Trace changes to records before proposing bounded commitments, deferrals or reductions in fixed expense. Forecasts include assumptions, baseline and scenarios; estimated spending is not a payment. Save revisions or reports through current contracts, reuse receipts and let the service maintain snapshot cursors.
